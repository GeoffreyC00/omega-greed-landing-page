Motorcycle Shot List Checklist

Print or use on your phone before every content ride.

OmegaGreed · Bonus PDF

Pre-Ride Shots

  • Gear laid out / getting geared up
  • Helmet on (closeup + medium)
  • Gloves
  • Boots
  • Jacket / riding gear details
  • Bike startup sequence
  • Cold start (if relevant)
  • Garage or parking setup
  • Loading gear on the bike
  • Tank bag / camera mount closeups
  • Quick audio check — mic levels
  • “Recording” confirmation on each camera

Riding Shots

  • POV handlebar or chest mount
  • Insta360 pointed back at rider
  • Insta360 side / front angles
  • Road signs / route markers
  • Lane changes / city riding
  • Highway / open road

Riding Shots (continued)

  • Gas station stop (pull in, fill up, leave)
  • Scenic road sections
  • Riding through interesting areas
  • Speed / motion B-roll (safe, legal stretches)

Destination Shots

  • Establishing wide shot of location
  • Bike parked — hero angle
  • Walkaround of bike at location
  • Drone establishing (if legal, safe, allowed)
  • Beauty / landscape shots
  • Talking update to camera
  • Environment details
  • Departure — leaving the stop
Shot List Checklist · Page 3

Story Shots

  • Something goes wrong — problem moment
  • Getting lost / wrong turn
  • Dropping the bike (film recovery if it happens)
  • Bad weather rolling in
  • Delays (traffic, breakdown, waiting)
  • Recovery — fixing, adapting, continuing
  • Emotional reflection (honest update)
  • “What changed from beginning to now?” moment

Thumbnail Shots

  • Bike + rider + environment — clean composition
  • Strong facial expression or action pose
  • Vertical crop safe for Shorts/Reels cover
  • Multiple options — do not rely on one frame
  • Shot before light fades or you are exhausted
Shot List Checklist · Page 4

Short-Form Shots

  • Vertical POV clip (15–60 sec idea)
  • Quick talking hook to camera
  • One satisfying visual moment (sound + motion)
  • Before/after or problem/solution clip
  • Gear detail macro shot
  • “Day in the life” montage beats (3–5 quick clips)
Run this list every content ride. Missing shots are harder to fix in post than on the road.
