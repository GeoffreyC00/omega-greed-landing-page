Audio

Wireless mic is non-negotiable for talking segments. Wind noise ruins riding clips without a real mic. Test levels before the ride. Bring wind protection.

Mounts

  • Handlebar clamp (POV) · Chest or backpack clip
  • Magnetic cap clip · Quick-release adapters
  • Invisible selfie stick (360) · Tripod / mini tripod

Rule: Bring the mount you planned for — and one backup.

Drones · Filters · Storage

Drones: Arrival establishing shots, scenic overlooks. Do not use while riding. Know local laws.

ND filters: Essential for bright daylight riding. Reduces harsh sky blowout. Match size to each camera.

Storage: High-speed microSD for action/360. Offload to external SSD nightly on trips. Duplicate before deleting cards.

Phone Gear

  • Lens clip-on kits for quick clips
  • Phone mount (vibration-safe)
  • Phone mic adapter
