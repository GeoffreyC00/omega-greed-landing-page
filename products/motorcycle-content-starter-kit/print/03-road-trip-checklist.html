Road Trip Creator Checklist

Multi-day shoots · destination videos · long haul content days

OmegaGreed · Bonus PDF

Cameras

  • Action camera + mounts
  • 360 camera (if using)
  • Pocket / gimbal camera
  • Main camera body + lens
  • Drone + controller + props
  • Phone (backup + short-form)

Audio

  • Wireless mic transmitter(s)
  • Receiver / phone adapter
  • Windscreen / deadcat
  • Spare mic batteries
  • Test recording before leaving

Mounts & Support

  • Handlebar clamp
  • Chest / backpack mount
  • Quick-release adapters
  • Selfie stick / invisible stick
  • Tripod or mini tripod
  • Suction / alternative mount (backup)
Road Trip Checklist · Page 2

Power & Media

  • Camera batteries (all bodies)
  • USB-C / charging cables
  • Power bank
  • Car charger / multi-port USB
  • Memory cards (formatted)
  • Card reader or laptop

Lenses & Filters

  • Primary lens(es)
  • ND filters (all camera sizes)
  • Polarizer if needed
  • Lens cloth

Riding Gear

  • Helmet (with comm if used)
  • Jacket, gloves, boots
  • Hydration / snacks
  • Layer for cold / rain
Road Trip Checklist · Page 3

Emergency & Road

  • Tire repair / pump (if touring)
  • Basic tool kit
  • First aid
  • Insurance / registration accessible
  • Route downloaded offline

Content Planning

  • Mission written (one sentence)
  • Shot list printed or on phone
  • Thumbnail plan (when + where)
  • Story beats outlined (beginning / middle / end)
  • Project folder created on drive

Storage Workflow

  • External hard drive packed
  • Nightly card offload plan
  • Folder structure ready (see Template PDF)
  • Editor notes doc started
