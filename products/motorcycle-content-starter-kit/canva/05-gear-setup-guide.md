## CAMERAS

**Action camera (POV / mounted)**  
Primary use: riding footage, handlebar angles, durability.  
Examples: DJI Osmo Action 5 Pro, GoPro-class bodies.

**360 camera**  
Primary use: rider-facing angles, immersive clips, invisible stick shots.  
Examples: Insta360 X4/X5.

**Pocket / gimbal camera**  
Primary use: cinematic B-roll, walk-and-talk, stable handheld.  
Examples: DJI Pocket 3.

**Mirrorless / main camera**  
Primary use: highest quality A-roll, thumbnails, sponsored segments.  
Examples: Sony A7S III.

**Drone**  
Primary use: establishing shots, scenic road context (follow all laws).  
Examples: DJI Mini 3 Pro.

**Phone**  
Primary use: backup, short-form, quick updates, hyperlapse.

---

## AUDIO

**Wireless mic system**  
Non-negotiable for talking segments. Wind noise ruins riding clips without a real mic.

**Tips:**  
- Test levels before the ride  
- Bring wind protection  
- Record room tone / ambient at stops if needed  

---

## MOUNTS

- Handlebar clamp (POV)  
- Chest or backpack clip  
- Magnetic cap clip (lightweight POV)  
- Quick-release adapters (swap fast between mounts)  
- Invisible selfie stick (360)  
- Tripod / mini tripod (stops and destinations)  

**Rule:** Bring the mount you planned for — and one backup option.

---

## DRONES

Use for: arrival establishing shots, scenic overlooks, parking lot reveals.  
Do not use while riding. Know local laws and venue rules.

---

## ND FILTERS

Essential for bright daylight riding footage. Reduces harsh sky blowout and keeps shutter natural. Match filter size to each camera body.

---

## STORAGE

- High-speed microSD for action/360  
- Offload to external SSD nightly on trips  
- Duplicate backups before deleting cards  

---

## PHONE GEAR

- Lens clip-on kits for quick clips  
- Phone mount for tank or bar (vibration-safe)  
- Rode/Videomic-style phone mic adapter
