## PART 1 — THE OMEGAGREED PHILOSOPHY

### Why I make videos
- Documenting your life matters
- Not every video needs to go viral
- Content = living journal; future you / family may care more than algorithms
- Experience mattered even when nobody watched

### Why I ride
- Last truly analog experience
- Environment: weather, smell, road, temperature
- Notice what drivers miss

### Why I film rides
- Privilege — nobody forces you; if you don't enjoy it, don't
- Unpublished footage still has value

### Why some rides become videos
- Not every ride deserves a video
- Need: purpose, story, lesson/challenge/transformation
- One-sentence video point before you leave

### The motorcycle is not the story
- Desert lost, Vegas ride home, first road trip, ghost town
- Bike = vehicle into story

## PART 3 — LESSONS I LEARNED THE HARD WAY

1. **I rode all day with no story** — random footage ≠ content
2. **I thought the camera was recording** — Mojave desert; verify every stop
3. **Bad audio ruins great footage** — mic test in garage
4. **Film the recovery** — BMW GS sand; problem + payoff
5. **Get thumbnail photos early** — before ride home, light gone
6. **Overpacking slows you down** — lightweight = more content
