Meet the person behind OmegaGreed

Geoffrey R. Crawford

I left Buffalo to pursue my own goals. The move was the decision. The work after it was learning how to follow through when nobody else was holding the plan.

Paid media is where that got practical. More than eight years of building campaigns taught me to turn a loose idea into a plan: name the outcome, pick the next action, and check whether it actually happened. A campaign doesn’t move because the idea was exciting. It moves because someone decided what this week is for.

OmegaGreed is that same habit on my own projects. A ride, a build, a video — each one starts as a bigger idea and becomes a short list of steps I can finish. The Hayabusa in the desert, the builds, the videos that make it onto the channel: those are projects I planned and followed through on.

That’s the work of this pilot. We pick one milestone you care about, write the plan, and meet three more times so the four weeks don’t disappear. I’m not offering paid creator coaching, and I’m not promising an income or a result I can’t know yet.
