Four weeks

What we do together

Bring the goal you keep coming back to—even if you don’t know where to start. Together, we’ll talk through what you want, what’s getting in the way, and what moving forward could look like.

Then we’ll choose a meaningful milestone for your next four weeks, build an actionable plan around your real life, and check in each week as you put it into practice.

Kickoff

One private 60-minute kickoff call

We talk through the bigger goal and choose one milestone for these four weeks.

Game plan

A personalized one-page action plan

After the kickoff, you get weekly actions built around your real schedule.

Check-ins

Three private 20-minute check-ins

In weeks 2, 3, and 4 we look at what you did, what got in the way, and the next action.

Final check-in

A progress review and next steps

The last check-in looks at what moved and what you want to do next.
